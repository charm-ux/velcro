Add `<sl-dialog>`, with Shoelace 2.20.1's dialog contract (exports `./dialog` and `./dialog/class`): `open`, `label`, `noHeader` and `modal`; `show()` and `hide()`; the `sl-show`, `sl-after-show`, `sl-hide`, `sl-after-hide`, `sl-initial-focus` and cancelable `sl-request-close` events; the default, `label`, `header-actions` and `footer` slots; the `base`, `overlay`, `panel`, `header`, `header-actions`, `title`, `close-button`, `close-button__base`, `body` and `footer` parts; the `--width`, `--header-spacing`, `--body-spacing` and `--footer-spacing` custom properties; and the `dialog.show`, `dialog.hide`, `dialog.denyClose`, `dialog.overlay.show` and `dialog.overlay.hide` animations from the animation registry. The focus trap, the body scroll lock and their helpers are internal ports of Shoelace's `modal`, `tabbable`, `active-elements`, `scroll` and `offset` modules, and Shoelace's `blurActiveElement` is now one internal module shared by the alert and the dialog. The dialog is built on the core base element (core's native-`<dialog>` component is not used); the inherited `ready` event remains. Shoelace's quirks are matched and listed on the docs page (for example, a dialog written in markup with `open` emits no show events and does not move focus). The `dir` read type stays core's `'ltr' | 'rtl' | 'auto'` (the setter accepts any string).
