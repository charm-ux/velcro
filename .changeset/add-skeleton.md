Add prototype `VelcroSkeleton`, registered as `sl-skeleton` through the native registration helper on top of the `@charm-ux/core` skeleton. It has the `effect` property (`none`, `sheen`, `pulse`; not reflected), `base` and `indicator` parts, and the `--border-radius`, `--color` and `--sheen-color` custom properties, and renders no ARIA attributes, as in Shoelace. The light theme gains `--sl-border-radius-medium` and `--sl-border-radius-circle`. Adds the `@charm-ux/velcro/skeleton` and `@charm-ux/velcro/skeleton/class` exports (the class entry also exports the `SlSkeleton` alias) and updates the third-party notices for adapted Shoelace (MIT) wording, structure and styles. Not implemented or unverified: the core `shape` and `animation` properties, the `skeleton-base` part and the `--skeleton-*` properties (inherited but with no effect), reduced-motion handling (neither Shoelace 2.20.1 nor Velcro changes it), screen reader behavior, visual parity beyond computed-style checks in Chromium, other browsers, RTL and a dark theme.
