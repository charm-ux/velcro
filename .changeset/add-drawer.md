Add `<sl-drawer>`, with Shoelace 2.20.1's drawer contract (exports `./drawer` and `./drawer/class`): `open`, `label`, `placement`, `contained`, `noHeader` and `modal`; `show()` and `hide()`; the `sl-show`, `sl-after-show`, `sl-hide`, `sl-after-hide`, `sl-initial-focus` and cancelable `sl-request-close` events; the default, `label`, `header-actions` and `footer` slots; the `base`, `overlay`, `panel`, `header`, `header-actions`, `title`, `close-button`, `close-button__base`, `body` and `footer` parts; the `--size`, `--header-spacing`, `--body-spacing` and `--footer-spacing` custom properties; and the `drawer.showTop`, `drawer.showEnd`, `drawer.showBottom`, `drawer.showStart`, matching `drawer.hide*`, `drawer.denyClose`, `drawer.overlay.show` and `drawer.overlay.hide` animations from the animation registry. The drawer reuses the dialog's internal focus trap and body scroll lock and is built on the core base element (core has no drawer); the inherited `ready` event remains. Shoelace's quirks are matched and listed on the docs page (for example, removing an open drawer leaves its focus trap active, and with `CloseWatcher` a drawer opened `contained` ignores Escape after `contained` is removed). The `dir` read type stays core's `'ltr' | 'rtl' | 'auto'` (the setter accepts any string).
