Add a prototype `VelcroCard`, registered as `sl-card` on top of the `@charm-ux/core` card: a `base` part around `image`, `header`, `body` and `footer` parts with matching slots (the default slot is the body), the `--border-color`, `--border-radius`, `--border-width` and `--padding` custom properties, and `card--has-image`, `card--has-header` and `card--has-footer` classes that show the optional sections. It declares no properties or methods of its own, as in Shoelace 2.20.1. It inherits the core `ready` event (emitted once, without bubbling, after the first render), which is a divergence because Shoelace's card emits no events. Two other differences come from the core base styles it keeps: the `hidden` attribute hides the card (Shoelace's `:host` display rule overrides the browser's default there), and shadow elements stay `border-box` even when the host sets another `box-sizing` (Shoelace's inherit it). The light theme gains `--sl-border-radius-medium`, `--sl-shadow-x-small` and `--sl-panel-background-color`. Not implemented or unverified: the inherited core `heading`, `subheading` and `media-position` properties (declared but with no effect), the core markup, `card-*` parts and `media`, `heading` and `subheading` slots (not rendered), the core card styles and `--card-*` custom properties (not applied), screen reader behavior, visual parity beyond computed-style checks in Chromium, other browsers, RTL, localization and a dark theme. Registration is browser-only. Adds the `@charm-ux/velcro/card` and `@charm-ux/velcro/card/class` exports (with the `SlCard` alias) and updates the third-party notices for adapted Shoelace (MIT) wording, structure and styles.
